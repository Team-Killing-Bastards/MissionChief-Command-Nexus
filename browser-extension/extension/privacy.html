Nexus data and sharing

Nexus helps with MissionChief UK mission selection, dispatch, transport, naming and personnel management. It runs on the www and police MissionChief UK domains.

Stored in your browser

The game-side settings, training registers and diagnostic history use MissionChief site storage. The extension stores custom requirement rules, your sharing choice, a random installation ID and an offline upload queue. These settings are not automatically copied to a new browser profile or a different store installation.

Optional Google sharing

Sharing is off until you enable it in Sharing & Sync. A saved choice is preserved on update. Enabling sharing sends your MissionChief username and player ID, random installation and session IDs, mission requirements, selected vehicle and station details, estimated journey distance and time when available, transport actions, game and Nexus activity, timestamps, browser and screen information, error details and performance measurements to the project owner's Google Apps Script service, Google Sheets and Drive archives.

This information supports the project's gameplay reports and fault investigation. Nexus does not intentionally record typed field values, passwords, authentication cookies, arbitrary request bodies or full page HTML. The bundled client collects no analytics on unrelated websites.

Pause and retention

Turning sharing off stops new collection and uploads. Existing queued events remain locally until their seven-day expiry or queue limits apply. The queue is capped at 10,000 events and approximately 8 MiB, so long outages can lose events. Records already received by the Google service are not deleted by pausing or uninstalling the extension. The supplied backend retains historical records and archives without an automatic deletion schedule.

The current private-development endpoint accepts uploads without authenticating the player. Player and device labels are not proof of identity. Authentication, server retention and a published privacy-policy/contact URL must be settled before wider distribution.
