## What this does

Nexus adds controls to the two supported MissionChief UK domains. The audited manifest requires Chrome 111 or newer. This does not establish Safari, mobile-browser or other-browser compatibility.

## Prerequisites

Desktop Chrome, a MissionChief account and backups of existing settings/register where available.

## Step by step

1. Join [Discord support](https://discord.com/invite/mXnTHuNfvu) if you need community help or access clarification.
2. Open [Nexus in the Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/missionchief-command-nexu/pheccockibcappcdgonjjfcmlkemmaln).
3. Check the displayed version, select **Add to Chrome** and review permissions.
4. Disable old Nexus userscripts and duplicate installations.
5. Sign in to the normal MissionChief website. Never enter your game password into the guide.
6. Refresh the game, open the extension popup and select **Open Nexus**.
7. Record the installed version in Chrome's extension management page.

## Expected result

Nexus appears once. The popup reports the installed build and game-tab state.

## Warnings

> Userscript and extension storage do not automatically transfer. Check your backup and migration options before removing the old installation.

A current Discord membership gate or approval sequence has not been verified. The invitation exists in source; further access steps are not invented here.

## Troubleshooting

If **Open Nexus** is unavailable, use a supported game tab and refresh after enabling the extension. If two interfaces appear, stop automation and disable duplicates. Include the installed version when reporting a mismatch with the guide.
