MissionChief Command Nexus 3.0.43.149 — Realism Map direct service toggles

Realism Map UI
- Built from 3.0.43.148.
- Removes the standalone “Realism Map” launcher button completely.
- Removes the collapsible/scrolling Realism Map menu.
- Shows every Realism service as its own always-visible toggle directly inside the native New building form.
- Active service toggles are highlighted blue; inactive supported services remain dark.
- Unsupported dataset types remain visible but disabled: Disaster Response, Lifeguard Stations and Doctors/Clinics.
- Keeps Clear all and Refresh actions beneath the service toggles.
- Existing saved service selections are restored automatically and their overlays reload when the New building form appears.

Behaviour preserved
- Uses the Nexus VPS/PostGIS viewport API.
- Pan/zoom continues to refresh selected overlays with debounce and bounded cache.
- Marker cleanup and request cancellation remain unchanged.
- No construction, purchasing or dispatch action is performed by the Realism Map controls.
- All 3.0.43.148 behaviour outside the Realism entry UI is preserved, including the per-mission Ambulance allocation limit.
