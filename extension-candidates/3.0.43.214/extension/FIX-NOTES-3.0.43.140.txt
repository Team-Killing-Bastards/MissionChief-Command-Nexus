- Planned rows with no trustworthy alliance/participation evidence fail closed and remain excluded rather than being guessed as shared.
- Captured rows are labelled `Planned appearance` in the Nexus Alliance missions panel.
- The existing Alliance panel MutationObserver now refreshes when MissionChief adds, removes or updates planned appearances.
- The existing participation verification through /api/vehicles remains unchanged.
- The existing exact Ocean-only/offshore mission filter from 3.0.43.139 remains active, including the pre-dispatch guard.
- Fire Station Open Day mission type 526 is not part of the Ocean-only exclusion set and remains eligible.
- MissionChief's native mission lists are not changed or hidden by Nexus.
