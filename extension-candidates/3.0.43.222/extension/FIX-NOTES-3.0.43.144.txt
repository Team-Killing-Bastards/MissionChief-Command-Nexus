New Mission control settings:
- Limit Ambulances per mission — default OFF.
- Maximum Ambulances per mission — default 20, range 1-99.

Locked behaviour:
- The cap applies only to ordinary MissionChief Ambulances, exact vehicle type 5.
- Ambulance Officer, Mass Casualty Equipment, HEMS and every other EMS type are outside the cap.
- The cap is enforced in fresh Unit Finder, patient Ambulance selection, late-patient recovery, Mission Update and Auto Mode through the shared vehicle-selection path.
- Nexus keeps a mission-lifetime ledger of unique Type-5 vehicle IDs in sessionStorage.
- Type-5 Ambulances already driving to or on the mission are absorbed into that ledger.
- Type-5 Ambulances selected for a Nexus/native dispatch are committed before the dispatch navigation.
- Once a Type-5 Ambulance has been committed to a mission, it keeps consuming one slot while transporting to hospital, while temporarily absent from the mission DOM, and when it later returns to the mission.
- Returning the same vehicle ID does not consume another slot.
- Each mission ID has its own allocation.
- The ledger is bounded to 160 missions and entries expire after 48 hours; MissionChief mission IDs are not reused, so stale entries cannot cap a different mission.
- Turning the setting OFF restores the existing uncapped behaviour immediately.

Threshold interaction:
- Ambulance Officer demand continues to be calculated from the original game/patient requirement before the cap.
- Mass Casualty Equipment now also retains the recorded uncapped ordinary Ambulance demand, so a mission requesting 77 Ambulances can still trigger the configured MCE rule even when the per-mission Ambulance cap is 20.

Example with limit 20:
- Mission requests 77 Ambulances -> Nexus selects at most 20 unique Type-5 Ambulances for the mission lifetime.
- If 6 of those 20 take patients to hospital and disappear from the mission vehicle tables, the allocation remains 20/20.
- If Auto Mode or Mission Update revisits while those 6 are away, Nexus sends 0 replacement Ambulances.
- When those same ambulance IDs return, they are recognised as the same 20 allocated units.
