Nexus 3.0.43.176 - MCE dispatch and patient support

Fixes the final dispatch coverage check ignoring the configured ambulance cap.
With 30 ambulances committed and a cap of 30, an outstanding patient ambulance
request no longer vetoes sending selected Mass Casualty Equipment. Missing MCE,
other vehicle requirements and trained-personnel shortages remain blocking.

Fresh patient-only missions now run configured support thresholds before being
marked ready, even when no general vehicle requirement rows were found.

Live inspection of mission 261791800 confirmed 30 ambulances and no MCE on scene,
no travelling units table, and available type-33 MCE units. No live dispatch was
performed. Regression tests reproduce the previous final-check failure and
verify the fix. The first dispatch for that mission was not in the supplied log.

Reload the extension, then refresh the MissionChief game page before testing.
