# Community Code of Conduct

MissionChief Command Nexus welcomes technical discussion, testing evidence and constructive contributions.

## Expected behaviour

Participants should:

- Communicate clearly and respectfully.
- Critique ideas and code rather than individuals.
- Keep reports factual, reproducible and relevant.
- Respect privacy and remove sensitive information from public posts.
- Represent authorship and contributions accurately.
- Accept project-maintainer decisions without harassment or repeated disruption.

## Unacceptable behaviour

The following is not acceptable:

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks or discriminatory conduct.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Deliberately misleading bug reports or fabricated evidence.
- Spam, malicious files, credential collection or unsafe links.
- Attempts to pressure maintainers into unsafe releases or unreviewed code changes.
- Misrepresenting project ownership, development credit or contributor status.

## Enforcement

Organisation owners may edit, hide, lock or remove content and may restrict participation when behaviour damages the project or its community.

Reports should be made privately to an organisation owner when they contain sensitive information. Good-faith reports will be handled as discreetly as practical.
